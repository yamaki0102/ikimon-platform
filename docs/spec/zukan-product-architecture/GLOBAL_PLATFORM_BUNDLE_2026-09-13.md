## Adopted boundary

ZUKAN is a global bundle of peer public platforms/experiences, with no privileged default portal or mandatory general AI prompt. Environment, citizen records, biosecurity and regional knowledge remain first-class alongside jobs, food, events, offers and further public discovery profiles. The root may connect experiences but is not their parent application. Public contribution need not originate in NOCOSIL.

Adopt the normative design's locale-aware stable URL contract, Area/public identity separation, source/publication/representation/placement semantics, typed domain profiles, rights-safe public exchange, independent destination results, correction/expiry/withdrawal, global localization, discovery/index policy, operation-scoped cost boundary and GP01–GP30 acceptance.

This is a product-model correction, not only copy. The earlier default-app interpretation and Japan-only mandatory address/URL hierarchy are SUPERSEDED. Existing Knowledge Core / Participation / Experience responsibilities remain. `Taxon`, biological observations, Job terms and coupon redemption do not become one universal Listing Kernel.
