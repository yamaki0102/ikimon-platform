# Decision: simple ZUKAN, organizer/publication work in NOCOSIL

Date: 2026-09-05. Status: ADOPTED ON MERGE; implementation separate.

The owner explicitly requested that ZUKAN remain simple around images, discovery and participation, while NOCOSIL creates/manages organizer work. The same user-facing product must also display signboards, interviews, exhibitions, institutions and press releases without first modeling every business worldwide.

Decision: adopt [SPEC §17](../SPEC.md#17-simple-participation-and-public-information-boundary--2026-09-05), [PLAN](../PLAN.md) and [App Experience §17](../../zukan-app-experience/ZUKAN_APP_EXPERIENCE_V1.md#17-read-record-and-participate-organize-and-publish-in-nocosil--2026-09-05). NOCOSIL composes existing work/Actions/Public Surface; ZUKAN reuses regional knowledge and participation; authorized specialist providers own complex transactions.

KEEP existing Program/Event/Record/Source/Rights/Publication, free entitlements and working routes. CHANGE organizer UI ownership and partial-participation/communication contracts. DELETE new Opportunity/Event subsystem, compulsory event objects for content, exhaustive facility inventory and duplicate press CMS. DEFER native complex ticketing, shared identity adoption and unproven broad connectors.

This is a refinement of the existing broad horizon, not a new vertical expansion. Source ownership and free rights do not move merely because the interaction surface does. The detailed rationale, primary evidence and counterexamples are in [management's final design](https://github.com/yamaki0102/all-projects-management/blob/main/operations/decisions/2026-09-05-zukan-nocosil-participation-graph-review-input-v1.md) and [acceptance](https://github.com/yamaki0102/all-projects-management/blob/main/operations/decisions/2026-09-05-participation-acceptance-v1.md); older drafts and prior §8 organizer-expansion interpretations are superseded only where incompatible. Existing independent §16 read-first decisions remain valid. No runtime, schema or authorization migration is performed by this decision.
