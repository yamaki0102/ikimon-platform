# ikimon.life Current Surface Contract

Purpose: keep public copy, product KPI, and internal strategy from mixing after the 2026-06 broad review.

## Decision

ikimon.life has one public entry message, one product north star, and one internal spine.

| Layer | Contract | Where it may appear |
|---|---|---|
| Public first touch | `ENJOY NATURE` / near-nature observation as a light, personal starting point | public landing, nav, onboarding, observation UI |
| Product north star | active places: places revisited by local users with repeat records | KPI docs, ops dashboards, planning notes |
| Internal spine | `Place Intelligence OS` / evidence-backed place memory | strategy docs, partner/research planning, internal architecture |
| Enterprise offer | monitoring acceleration: turn local records into candidate/verified records, effort, gaps, and next monitoring plans | `/for-business/monitoring/apply`, partner docs, sales material |

## Non-Goals

- Do not make health, child education, TNFD, OECM, credits, and passive sensing equal public-top messages.
- Do not use `Place Intelligence OS` as ordinary public landing copy.
- Do not imply health improvement, certification, TNFD completion, rare species discovery, grant adoption, or specialist identification is guaranteed.

## Current Interpretation

Older v3.8 strategy documents remain useful as vision/history. They are not the current public surface source of truth when they conflict with:

1. `docs/strategy/ikimon_public_surface_canonical_pack_2026-04-22.md`
2. `docs/IKIMON_MASTER_STATUS_AND_PLAN_2026-04-12.md`
3. this contract

## Acceptance Checks

- `/` hero remains public-first and does not lead with internal-system language.
- `/for-business/monitoring/apply` names a concrete monitoring offer and keeps guarantee boundaries visible.
- KPI discussions can map public engagement back to active places.
- strategy docs may discuss broader futures only after the current public surface and monitoring offer are closed.
