# Growing local records together.

ZUKAN connects records of a place: nature, culture, history, work, daily life, and local activities.

From photos, sounds, and short notes, you learn how a place is now and what has been handed down. You revisit them later, check the evidence, and add the next record. It starts with one nearby record.

## Small finds become local records

A roadside flower, a bird call, preparations for a festival, an old signpost, local work and daily life, an activity people gathered for. Each one is a clue to the place.

You do not need to know the name, and a record does not need to be impressive. Keeping it as a photo or note has value. Comparing a street photo with an earlier season, checking the origin written on a sign, or looking back at activities in the same place gradually widens how records can be read.

## Look back by place and time

We care about when, where, and from what a record was made. When the date or source is unknown, it stays unknown; we do not fill the gap with guesses.

When new records are added, earlier photos and notes are not erased or replaced. The aim is to follow the same place over time. Seasonal changes in nature and changes in streets and daily life are read as layers of local time.

## Evidence and verification are kept separate

What a photo shows, what a sign or document says, and what you infer from the record are different things. Saving a record and having its content verified are also treated separately.

AI offers candidates and hints for telling things apart. Even when looking up a living thing, a name is never settled by an AI candidate alone. We value human checking and evidence, and we do not present unknowns or differing opinions as if they were confirmed.

## You choose what to make public

Saving a record and making it public are different. Check the range before publishing, and pay attention to location and to information contained in photos.

Photos showing people, stories you were told, and materials made by others need consent and a check of terms of use. A public record is not necessarily free to use for another purpose. Reading only, keeping a record private, and checking later are all valid choices.

## The idea of Enjoy Life

**Enjoy Life** means enjoying nearby discoveries and staying connected with them in daily life.

**Enjoy living things. Enjoy daily life. Enjoy the life of the Earth.**

Nature observation is one concrete way in. We offer features and reading to look up the names of flowers and birds and to revisit seasonal change. In the same way, we pay attention to culture, history, work, daily life, and local activities, and do not narrow a place to a single field.

## Start from a nearby place

You do not need perfect knowledge to begin. Reading records of a place you care about, or saving one photo or note of your own, is a way into your community.
